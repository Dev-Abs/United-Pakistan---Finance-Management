## Product goal and architecture

A responsive Express/vanilla-JavaScript finance management PWA migrating from Google Sheets to Supabase Postgres and sector-based multi-tenancy. It manages monthly member funds, payments, expenses, follow-ups, reports, settings, role-based access, and campaign-based special funds. `public/` is the SPA, `server/` is the Express API, `mobile/` is the Flutter client, and Vercel serves Express. Phases 1–3 are complete and verified against the live database. Phase 4 AI sector scoping is implemented and regression-tested; review is required before Phase 5 cleanup/docs.
